## What this demo is

A complete, working copy of the PRISM frontend, running entirely in your
browser with **no backend**. It boots in demo mode against an in-browser
simulator.

- **The fleet is synthetic.** Every robot, site, mission, alert, and telemetry
  reading you see was generated in your browser moments ago. No real robot,
  customer, or organisation is represented.
- **The financial figures are modelled, not measured.** ROI, payback, NPV, cost
  per action, and value-per-action are computed from illustrative default
  coefficients shipped with the app. They are a modelling tool, not a forecast,
  a quotation, or financial advice.
- **Compliance and safety content is illustrative.** The regulatory rulesets
  reference real standards, and the numeric thresholds in them are conservative
  defaults, not a substitute for a site risk assessment. Nothing here is a
  certified safety system, and no output should be relied on for a decision
  affecting human safety or regulatory compliance.
- **Nothing you enter is transmitted.** Anything you type stays in your own
  browser's local storage. Clear your browser data and it is gone. There is no
  account, and no server receives your input.

Web fonts are requested from Google's CDN, so your IP address reaches Google on
page load, as it would on most of the web. That is the only third-party request
the demo makes.
